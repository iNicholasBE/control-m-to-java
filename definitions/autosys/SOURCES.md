## Not confirmed from a public page

- `sendevent -E SET_GLOBAL -G "NAME=value"` for setting the business date global (comment in the JIL). Verify against your release.
- Using a renewable resource with `amount: 1` as an exclusive lock is our modelling choice, built from the documented resource semantics. AutoSys has no separate lock object in JIL.
- SLA prediction: `must_complete_times` alarms after the fact. Broadcom sells prediction in separate products; nothing in JIL predicts a breach.
